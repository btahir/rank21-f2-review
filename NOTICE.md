# Attribution and rights

This repository is a public research draft made available for review. The original project text and code (everything outside the clearly marked upstream material) have not yet been assigned an open-source license; until one is, they are provided for reading and verification only, with all other rights reserved. Author attribution and licensing decisions remain to be finalized. The repository's existence is not an arXiv submission or peer-reviewed publication.

The mathematical restriction catalog and published baseline certificates come from Chengu Wang, *Automated Lower Bounds for Bilinear Complexity over Finite Fields*, arXiv:2603.07280v11. The pinned upstream source is [wcgbg/tensor-rank-lower-bound](https://github.com/wcgbg/tensor-rank-lower-bound/tree/0ab0562f2fb5430e3ce16035e5882720b5bb613b), distributed under its MIT license. Preserve the corresponding upstream license wherever its material is included. Pinned source URLs and SHA-256 values are recorded with the verification evidence.

The global proof certificates, strengthened restricted-bound claims, packaging and draft were produced in a local LLM-assisted research campaign building on that work. The independent replay code in `verification/restricted/` and the audit record in `docs/audits/` were produced by a separate LLM audit on 7 September 2026. Neither Wang nor other cited researchers have endorsed or reviewed this candidate merely by being cited.

The Laderman factor table used for positive controls in the original lab is not copied into this repository because a redistribution license for that particular third-party file was not established. Laderman's mathematical upper bound is cited in the paper. The external Beuchert implementation is also not included; the documented coverage defect and small corrected replay are local observations, not a new source-code distribution.
